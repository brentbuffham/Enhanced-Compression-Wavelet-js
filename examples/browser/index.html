Each pixel is one 64×64 block, coloured by its compressed size. Big blocks hold photo detail; tiny ones are empty background.

Tries the optional post-steps on the current picture.

Click to add points, double-click to finish. Points are in map coordinates.

Pick colour, then click the map: it reads that pixel at full resolution. Pixels within below..above of it on every channel become transparent.

Decodes pixels with readRegion. x, y, width and height are full-resolution pixels; the library picks the coarsest level that is still sharp enough for outWidth × outHeight. With resample "nearest" (what QGIS and GDAL do) the output matches the ECW SDK bit for bit; "average" box-averages the decoded level for a smoother, softer look.

Browser storage (OPFS) used by ecw-js. Kept files reopen instantly after a reload; scratch files hold decoded output and are swept after 24 hours; the tile cache keeps reconstructed tiles so revisited views decode fast, even after a reload.
